Digital Media Club

Where Stories Meet Pixels

We are a creative hub for storytellers, designers, and strategists passionate about shaping the digital world through content, design, and social media.

Who We Are

About the Club

Explore our vision, mission, and the roles we play in shaping the digital presence of our campus.

Our Vision

To create a vibrant digital ecosystem on campus that tells the story of ISAC, celebrates student achievements, and builds a strong online community.

Our Mission

Empower students with digital media skills, produce compelling content that amplifies campus life, and maintain a professional digital presence across all platforms.

Our Roles

  • Maintain & update ISAC and college websites.
  • Manage official college social media handles (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, X).
  • Create compelling content — photography, videography, and graphic design.
  • Promote campus events, achievements, and news across digital platforms.
What We Do

Our Key Responsibilities

The Digital Media Club drives the online presence of ISAC through these core functions.

Website Management

Maintaining and updating the ISAC and college websites to ensure they remain current, functional, and visually engaging for students and visitors.

Social Media Management

Managing official college social media handles — Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, and X — to build a strong and engaging online community.

Content Creation

Producing high-quality photography, videography, and graphic design content that showcases campus life, events, and student achievements.

Promotion & Outreach

Promoting campus events, achievements, and news across all digital platforms to increase visibility and engagement among students and the wider community.
